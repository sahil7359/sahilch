# Yardstick

## Is Sahil working on anything right now?
Sahil is currently building Yardstick, an agent that trains candidate models on tabular data and reports honestly how good they actually are. [interview/project3]
It features automated leakage detection, comparison of every model against a trivial baseline, and metric selection that is justified rather than defaulted. [interview/project3]
It is in progress and not yet released. [interview/project3]

## What will Yardstick use?
Yardstick is planned to use Python, scikit-learn, MLflow, LangGraph, FastAPI, and Docker. [interview/project3]

## When will Yardstick be finished?
Yardstick is in progress; no completion date or results are being claimed yet. [interview/project3]
